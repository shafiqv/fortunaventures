Who Am I

The person behind the search

Shafiq Vallani
LinkedIn ↗

My path to buying a business started well before adolescence. My grandfather immigrated to Canada in the 1970s and built a blue-collar hardware and lumber business from scratch. I spent much of my childhood running around the store and yard, watching what ownership actually looked like up close: the grind of it, but also the pride that comes along with it - employees building careers and families, and customers who kept coming back because they were known by name.

Since then, I've worked across venture capital (TELUS Global Ventures and Redesign Health), buy-side M&A (RBC Ventures), and operations (Carpedia International and BDC), along with capital markets roles at Red Cloud Securities and League Assets. Along the way I also founded and led two companies as CEO - Gryphon Sports Management, which I grew to profitability before a majority stake sale in 2021, and ISBY, which I built and exited.

I also managed to find an amazing wife who somehow still puts up with my spreadsheets.

Outside of work, I stay active across cycling, tennis, and hockey.

If any of this resonates, let's chat.

How This Works

From first conversation to close

A straightforward, confidential process. Most conversations start well before any formal process - often just an exploratory coffee.

Introductory conversation

A no-obligation call to understand your business, your timeline, and what matters most to you in a transition - whether you're actively exploring a sale or simply curious.

Mutual NDA & information exchange

High-level financials and operating details shared under a signed NDA to assess fit against acquisition criteria.

Indication of interest

A non-binding, preliminary view on valuation and structure if there's a fit - fast, and without dragging you through a lengthy process on a deal that isn't real.

Letter of intent

Agreed terms, exclusivity, and a defined diligence timeline - with financing structured well ahead of time, so it isn't a question mark late in the process.

Diligence & close

Focused, respectful diligence built around your team's bandwidth, followed by a transition plan you help shape.
